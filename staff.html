LIBRARY LEADERSHIP

Role of the University Librarian

The University Librarian provides professional leadership for the University Library and coordinates library services in support of teaching, learning and research.

The office also supports the development and effective management of library collections, electronic resources, information services and research support.

LIBRARY ADMINISTRATION

Our Commitment

The Library is committed to providing an effective information environment that supports students, researchers and members of the University community.

Library services continue to evolve alongside developments in information technology and scholarly communication.
