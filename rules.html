LIBRARY POLICIES

Policies, Rules & Regulations

Guidelines that promote responsible use of the University Library, its facilities, collections and electronic resources.

Library User Guidelines

All library users are expected to use library facilities, collections and information resources responsibly and respectfully.

GENERAL RULES

Use of the Library

  • Maintain a quiet and suitable environment for study and research.
  • Treat library staff, users, furniture and equipment with respect.
  • Keep mobile devices on silent mode where appropriate.
  • Keep the library environment clean.
  • Follow reasonable instructions given by authorized library staff.
BORROWING

Borrowing & Returning Materials

  • Library materials should be borrowed using the approved library procedure.
  • Users are responsible for materials issued to them.
  • Borrowed materials should be returned within the permitted period.
  • Users should report lost or damaged materials to the Library.
  • Borrowing privileges may be subject to applicable library regulations.
DIGITAL RESOURCES

Electronic Resource Use

Users of the Digital Library must comply with applicable licence conditions and responsible-use requirements.

💻

Authorized Use

Electronic resources should be used for legitimate teaching, learning and research purposes.

🔐

Account Security

Users should protect their institutional login credentials and should not share them with unauthorized persons.

©️

Copyright

Users should respect copyright, licence restrictions and the terms governing electronic resources.

USER CONDUCT

Expected Behaviour

  • Users should maintain appropriate conduct within library premises.
  • Harassment, intimidation or disruptive behaviour should not be tolerated.
  • Library furniture and equipment should be used carefully.
  • Food and drinks should only be taken into areas where permitted.
  • Users should respect the study needs and privacy of other library users.
ACADEMIC INTEGRITY

Copyright, Plagiarism & Responsible Research

Use of Information

Information obtained from books, journals, databases, websites and other sources should be properly acknowledged using appropriate citation practices.

Users are responsible for evaluating information before using it in academic work.

Academic Integrity

Library resources should support honest academic work. Users should avoid plagiarism, fabrication, falsification and other forms of academic misconduct.

Where applicable, users should follow the University's academic integrity requirements.

ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

Ethical Use of Artificial Intelligence in Academic Work

AI tools may support learning and research, but users remain responsible for the accuracy, originality and integrity of their academic work.

Responsible Use

  • Verify information generated by AI systems.
  • Do not present unverified AI-generated content as established fact.
  • Protect confidential, personal and sensitive information when using AI tools.
  • Follow relevant institutional requirements concerning AI-assisted academic work.

Academic Accountability

  • The student or researcher remains responsible for submitted academic work.
  • AI assistance should not replace critical thinking and scholarly judgement.
  • AI-generated references and citations should be independently verified.
  • Users should acknowledge AI assistance where required by applicable academic guidelines.
