FACULTY LIBRARIES

Faculty Libraries & Collections

Library collections are organised to support the teaching, learning and research needs of the University's faculties and colleges.

MEDICAL & HEALTH SCIENCES

College of Medical & Health Sciences

Collections supporting Medicine and Surgery, Medical Laboratory Science, Physiotherapy, Public Health, Radiography, Anatomy, Physiology and related health sciences.

Key areas: Medicine, Surgery, Medical Laboratory Science, Physiotherapy, Public Health, Radiography, Anatomy and Physiology.

NURSING

Faculty of Nursing Sciences

Nursing collections supporting undergraduate nursing education, clinical practice, health promotion and nursing research.

Key area: Nursing Sciences.

PHARMACY

Faculty of Pharmacy

Pharmaceutical and healthcare collections supporting teaching, professional training and research in pharmacy.

Key area: Pharmacy and Pharmaceutical Sciences.

COMPUTING & ENGINEERING

Faculty of Computing & Engineering

Collections supporting computing, information technology, cybersecurity, software development and engineering disciplines.

Key areas: Computer Science, Cybersecurity, Software Engineering, Computer Engineering, Civil Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Mechatronics Engineering and Electrical & Electronics Engineering.

BUSINESS & SOCIAL SCIENCES

Faculty of Business & Social Sciences

Collections supporting business, management, economics, communication, international relations and transport-related studies.

Key areas: Accounting, Economics, Mass Communication, International Relations and Diplomacy, and Transport, Logistics and Supply Chain Management.

LAW

Faculty of Law

Legal collections supporting undergraduate legal education, legal research and the study of Nigerian and international law.

Key area: Law.
