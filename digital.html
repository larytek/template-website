OPEN EDUCATIONAL RESOURCES

Learn with Open Resources

Open Educational Resources (OER) are educational materials that can be freely accessed and, depending on their licence, reused or adapted for learning and teaching.

Students should always check the licence and usage conditions of materials before copying, modifying or redistributing them.

RESPONSIBLE USE

Responsible Use of Digital Resources

Students and Researchers Should

  • Use library resources for legitimate academic purposes.
  • Respect copyright and licence conditions.
  • Use accurate citations when using scholarly information.
  • Protect personal and institutional login credentials.

Users Should Not

  • Share institutional login credentials with unauthorized users.
  • Attempt to bypass access restrictions.
  • Download excessive quantities of licensed content.
  • Use library resources for unlawful activities.
